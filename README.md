# 📚 Attendance Percentage Calculator

Many colleges set a minimum attendance requirement, often around **75%**, to let students sit for their exams. This repository breaks down how that rule works and why it matters.

---

## 📊 Why Attendance Percentage Matters

Falling short of the required attendance can lead to:

- ❌ Exam eligibility issues
- ❌ Grade deductions
- ❌ Formal attendance notices

---

## 📐 The Math Behind It

To stay eligible, your attendance percentage needs to meet:

```
(Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100 ≥ Required Percentage
```

Skipping classes early in the semester makes it harder to recover later.
